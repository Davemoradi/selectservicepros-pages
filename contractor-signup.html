Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to join?
There is no monthly fee to join. Each lead has a price shown before acceptance, and you are charged only when you affirmatively accept that lead.
Am I charged if I pass on a lead?
No. Passing on a lead does not charge your wallet.
Do I need a trade license?
Licensing requirements depend on the work and jurisdiction. SSP currently collects license documentation during onboarding for HVAC, Plumbing, and Electrical. You remain responsible for every license or certification required by law for the services you perform.
What is the Founding 25 credit?
Eligible Founding 25 Houston contractors receive $250 in SSP promotional lead credit. Promotional credit is used for lead fees and is not refundable for cash.
